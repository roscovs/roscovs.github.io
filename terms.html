  • We need a flat, level, indoor area (or covered area if outdoors) of the size we tell you, and access to a standard power outlet within 5 metres.
  • We arrive before your booked time to set up. Booked hours are counted from when the booth or coverage starts, not from setup.
  • For safety, we may stop operating outdoors in rain, strong wind or extreme heat. Time lost because the venue is unsuitable or unavailable is not refundable.
