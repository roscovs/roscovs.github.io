FAQs

Quick answers about booking, prices, setup and delivery.

Can't find your answer? Call 0461 277 549 or send us an enquiry.

How do I book Rosco for my event?

Send us an enquiry on the contact page with your date, venue and the service you want. We'll check availability and send you a quote.

Your date is only secured once we receive your 30% deposit and confirm the booking in writing.

How much does it cost?

360 Photo Booth packages start at $399 for 3 hours. Photo Booth packages start at $449 for 2 hours. Visuals photography starts at $450 for 2 hours, and we can quote for custom coverage.

See the 360 Booth, Photo Booth and Visuals pages for every package.

Do you travel outside Melbourne?

Yes. We're based in Melbourne and cover all of Victoria. Travel fees may apply depending on your location. We'll include them in your quote before you book. See areas we serve.

Are setup and pack-down included?

Yes. Every package includes delivery, setup and pack-down. We arrive before your booked time to set up, and your hours start when the booth or coverage starts, not during setup.

How much space do you need?

We need a flat, level area and a standard power outlet within 5 metres. We'll tell you the exact space for your package when we send your quote.

Can the booth be set up outdoors?

Yes, as long as the area is covered. For safety, we may pause outdoors in rain, strong wind or extreme heat.

Is someone there to run the booth?

Yes. A Rosco attendant sets up and runs the booth for the whole event and helps your guests, so you can enjoy the night.

When do we get our photos and videos?

360 videos and booth photos are shared with guests on the night when your package includes it. Edited Visuals photos are usually delivered within 7 days. Your quote will give the delivery time for films and larger packages.

Please download and back up your files, because galleries stay online for a limited time.

Can I book more than one service?

Yes. Many events pair a booth with photography or video. Tick each service you want on the enquiry form and we'll quote them together.

What if I need to change the date or cancel?

If you tell us at least 14 days before the event, we'll move your booking to a new date once at no cost, subject to availability. Your deposit carries over.

If you cancel, the deposit is non-refundable. If you cancel within 7 days of the event, the full amount is payable. Read the full terms and conditions.
