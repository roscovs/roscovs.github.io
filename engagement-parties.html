FAQs

Questions

Can't find your answer? Call 0461 277 549 or message us.

Can you put our names and date on the photos and videos?

Yes. The 360 Signature and Ultimate packages include a custom overlay, and every Photo Booth package includes a custom overlay and, with Prints and All Night, a custom print design with your names, date or theme.

Can we pick a backdrop to match our theme?

Yes, with the Photo Booth Prints and All Night packages. Pick one to match your venue or colours, from sequins to flower walls.

Can we see every photo after the party?

Yes. The Photo Booth All Night package includes an online gallery of every photo, and the 360 Signature and Ultimate packages include an online gallery of every video.

How much space do you need?

We need a flat, level area and a standard power outlet within 5 metres. We'll tell you the exact space for your package when we send your quote.

Can the booth be set up outdoors?

Yes, as long as the area is covered. For safety, we may pause outdoors in rain, strong wind or extreme heat.

Do you travel outside Melbourne?

We're based in Melbourne and cover all of Victoria. Travel fees may apply depending on your location, and we include them in your quote before you book.

How do I lock in my date?

Send an enquiry with your date and venue and we'll send a quote, usually within one business day. Your date is secured once you pay a 30% deposit by PayID and we confirm in writing. The balance is due on the day.
