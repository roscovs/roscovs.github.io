FAQs

Travel questions

Planning a wedding, corporate event or party? See what suits your event.

Do you travel to my town?

Yes. We're based in Melbourne and cover all of Victoria, including the suburbs and towns on this page and everywhere in between. Tell us your venue or suburb when you enquire.

Is there a travel fee?

Travel fees may apply depending on your location. We include any travel fee in your quote before you book, so there are no surprises on the day.

Is setup included for regional events?

Yes. Every package includes delivery, setup and pack-down, wherever your event is. We arrive before your booked time, and your hours start when the booth or coverage starts.

Can you cover more than one service at a regional event?

Yes. Many events pair a booth with photography or video. Tick each service on the enquiry form and we'll quote them together, including travel.
