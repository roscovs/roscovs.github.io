Rosco / About us

One crew. Three ways to capture your night.

Rosco is a Melbourne events team behind the Rosco 360 Photo Booth, Rosco Photo Booth and Rosco Visuals. We bring the gear, run the night and hand you memories worth keeping.

Our story

Made for the moments people talk about

Rosco started with a simple idea: the best part of any event is the people in it, so give them a reason to step in front of the camera.

Today we cover weddings, birthdays, school formals and corporate events right across Victoria. Whether it's a spin on the 360 booth, a strip of prints to take home or a full photo and video package, every booking is set up, run and packed down by our own team.

You focus on the celebration. We take care of the lights, the camera and the queue.
